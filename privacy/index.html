PRIVACY

Privacy at GapYears Lab

GapYears Lab is designed to help people explore financial decisions without requiring them to upload their financial plans.

The Income Planner performs calculations in your browser and saves your plans locally when browser storage is available. This notice explains what is stored, what service providers may process, and the limits of browser-based storage.

Financial information entered into the Income Planner

You manually enter financial assumptions and planning information. Calculations run in your browser, including browser-based workers. No brokerage connection or in-app account is required. Any separate hosting or restricted-access authentication is distinct from an application account.

The reviewed application has no identified financial-data backend. Source-code analysis and local runtime testing found no automatic financial-data upload path. A subsequently reported live Chrome DevTools test using synthetic financial information showed no financial-data uploads during the tested workflow.

These findings describe the reviewed implementation and tested behavior, not a guarantee about all workflows, future changes, browser extensions, or compromised devices. Browser storage may retain your plans between visits.

Saving and exporting plans

Plans autosave using your browser’s localStorage when storage is available. Saved plans are not encrypted by the application. Storage can be unavailable or fail, and clearing browser data can delete saved plans.

Exported JSON files contain unencrypted financial information. Imported JSON files are read locally from files you choose. Protect downloaded exports and backups, and consider who can access shared computers and browser profiles. Permitted browser extensions or compromised devices can also affect the privacy or availability of stored information.

Clearing financial information

In the Income Planner, use:

Income Planner → Manage Plans → Local storage, privacy and backups → Clear Local Plans

When successful, this removes saved plan entries from the current browser origin. Browser site-data controls offer another way to remove local storage, subject to browser behavior.

  • It does not securely erase downloaded exports or backups.
  • It does not clear other browser profiles or devices.
  • It does not guarantee removal from other open tabs or browser memory.

Clearing local plans is not secure erasure. Protect or delete exported files and backups separately.

Website analytics

The public informational website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure website traffic and performance. Analytics may process visitor, device-related, and performance information; this is distinct from financial planning inputs.

Reported live browser testing confirmed the website’s analytics script loaded and performance requests were submitted. No Cloudflare Web Analytics script or analytics requests were observed from the separate Income Planner during the tested session. That observation does not guarantee every future session or configuration will be the same.

The website’s analytics code does not attach financial inputs, saved plans, or form contents to analytics events. We do not claim that Cloudflare processes no identifying or device-related information.

Hosting and infrastructure

GapYears Lab uses Cloudflare for website and application hosting. Hosting, security, and any access services may process ordinary request information, such as IP addresses, request metadata, and technical information needed to operate the services. This is separate from the planner’s financial inputs and local calculations.

The review did not independently verify every Cloudflare infrastructure setting, log retention policy, or external feedback-form configuration. This notice does not state unverified retention periods, legal-compliance assurances, or absolute confidentiality.

External services and feedback

The website or planner may link to external resources or feedback forms, which operate under their own privacy practices. The reviewed Income Planner does not automatically attach financial plans to those links.

Information you voluntarily submit to an external service leaves the planner’s local workflow. Do not send sensitive financial information or exported plan files through feedback forms.

Changes to privacy practices

This notice may be updated as GapYears Lab evolves, particularly if future features introduce accounts, cloud synchronization, subscriptions, or additional services. Those possibilities do not describe features being introduced by this notice.

Privacy questions

If you have questions about how GapYears Lab handles information, please contact hello@gapyearslab.com.

Please don't include sensitive financial information, account details, or exported financial plans in your email. See the Contact page for general questions and feedback.
