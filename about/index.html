ABOUT THE LAB

About GapYears Lab

More room to think about what comes next.

Built around the years between.

GapYears Lab is a free-to-use early-retirement income-planning platform. Its aim is to make interconnected financial decisions easier to explore, starting with annual income planning.

Approachable tools and clear explanations can help people ask better questions about life after full-time work.

Starting with the GapYears Income Planner

Our first application focuses on income sources, estimated federal income taxes only, ACA premiums and premium tax credits, Roth conversion opportunities, and comparisons of annual financial outcomes.

Tax estimates cover federal income taxes only. State income taxes and payroll/self-employment taxes are not currently modeled.

The GapYears Income Planner is available as a separate application. This website explains its features and includes an illustrative annual scenario.

WHY THE LAB EXISTS

Built from practical questions.

GapYears Lab grew out of the practical challenges of navigating early retirement. Managing retirement income brought the connections between federal taxes, investment decisions, Roth conversions, and healthcare costs into focus.

The founder brings more than 20 years of professional experience in financial technology, revenue operations, and analytics, including leadership experience at Visa. That analytical background shapes the project’s emphasis on using data and scenario modeling to understand financial tradeoffs.

The purpose is to make useful planning tools accessible to others navigating similar decisions. GapYears Lab provides educational planning estimates, not individualized financial, tax, or investment advice.
