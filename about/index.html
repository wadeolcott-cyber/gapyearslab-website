About GapYears Lab

More room to think about what comes next.

Built around the years between.

GapYears Lab is a free-to-use early-retirement income-planning platform. Its aim is to make interconnected financial decisions easier to explore, starting with annual income planning.

Approachable tools and clear explanations can help people ask better questions about life after full-time work.

Starting with the GapYears Income Planner

Our first application focuses on income sources, estimated federal and applicable state income taxes, ACA premiums and premium tax credits, Roth conversion opportunities, and comparisons of annual financial outcomes.

This website previews those concepts. Integration of the existing application is still ahead.
