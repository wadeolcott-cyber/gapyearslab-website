What connects the decisions?

Illustrative modified adjusted gross income (MAGI)
$54,000

Simplified example: $44,000 of income plus a $10,000 taxable conversion, assuming no adjustments or other MAGI additions. Actual tax and ACA definitions and household circumstances matter.

  • Income taxes Income sources and taxable conversions affect estimated federal and applicable state income taxes.
  • ACA healthcare costs Household MAGI can affect premium tax credit eligibility and net premiums, alongside other eligibility rules.
  • Annual tradeoffs Compare estimated taxes, net healthcare costs, and overall annual financial outcomes across scenarios.

No calculated results shown. This website does not calculate tax, premiums, credits, savings, or recommendations. The example does not identify an optimal conversion amount.

What the application helps you explore

  • Income from different sources. Bring annual income choices into one scenario.
  • Estimated income taxes. Consider federal and applicable state income taxes.
  • ACA healthcare costs. Explore health insurance premiums and premium tax credits alongside income.
  • Roth conversion opportunities. Explore how taxable conversions interact with taxes and healthcare costs.
  • Scenario comparison. Compare overall estimated annual financial outcomes and tradeoffs.

A focused starting point

The Income Planner currently focuses on annual income planning. It does not provide comprehensive multi-year retirement projections, portfolio longevity calculations, or Social Security optimization.

Application integration is still ahead
