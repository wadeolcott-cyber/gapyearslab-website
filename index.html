THE YEARS BETWEEN ARE DIFFERENT

Retirement isn't
one single chapter.

The stretch between leaving work and drawing traditional retirement benefits comes with its own questions.

Your income changes shape.

Savings, part-time work, and future benefits may each play a role at different times.

Conversions have tradeoffs.

A taxable Roth conversion can change annual income, with consequences for income taxes and ACA premium tax credits.

The pieces affect each other.

Withdrawals can affect taxes and healthcare costs. A useful plan looks at the connections.
