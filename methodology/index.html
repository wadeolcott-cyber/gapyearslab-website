OUR APPROACH

Methodology

Clear assumptions make for better questions.

Website illustration, not application results

The annual preview uses made-up figures: $24,000 in part-time earnings, an $18,000 taxable traditional IRA withdrawal, and $2,000 in interest. These total $44,000 before a $10,000 taxable Roth conversion.

The $54,000 illustrative modified adjusted gross income (MAGI) assumes no adjustments or other MAGI additions. A Roth conversion transfers assets between accounts and is not extra spending cash. Actual tax and ACA MAGI definitions and household circumstances matter.

The preview does not calculate taxes, insurance premiums, premium tax credits, savings, or recommendations. It is not an optimized scenario.

Current application scope

The separate GapYears Income Planner focuses on annual income sources, estimated federal and applicable state income taxes, ACA premiums and premium tax credits, Roth conversion opportunities, and scenario comparison. It does not provide comprehensive multi-year retirement projections, portfolio longevity calculations, or Social Security optimization.

Application methodology: documentation pending

Application documentation still needs to cover the application’s supported tax years and states, calculation methods, ACA eligibility assumptions, data sources, limitations, and update practices. This website illustration does not validate the application’s calculations.
